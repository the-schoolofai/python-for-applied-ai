3. Set the location filter to **Pakistan**, then run the same searches with the filter set to **remote**.
4. Find five real adverts. Not articles about jobs — actual adverts with an employer attached.

> **What counts as a real advert:** it names an employer or a platform, it says what the person would do, and you could click Apply. A news article about AI jobs is not an advert. A list of "top 10 AI careers" is not an advert.

## Done properly means

- **Every advert names an employer or a platform.** A job title with no company behind it is not evidence that the job exists.
- **The skill column says something specific** — n8n, prompt writing, content strategy, Google Sheets. Writing "AI" in that column tells you nothing you did not already know.
- **Your comparison notices something concrete.** *"Claude gave four sentences instead of five"* or *"only Gemini used a Pakistani example"* is a real observation. *"Claude is better"* is not, and you will not be able to defend it tomorrow.
- **The three verdict lines are yours.** If you cannot say them out loud without reading the page, they are not yours yet — write them again in your own words.

> Tomorrow opens with two minutes on Part B. Be ready to say which tool used a local example, and to disagree with someone who found the opposite.
